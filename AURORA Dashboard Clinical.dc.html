Model Facts — AI-COA (Deliberate AI)
Intended use: supplementary severity context alongside PHQ-9/HAM-D — never a replacement for clinician judgment or patient report.
Output: severity pseudo-sumscore with uncertainty interval, from behavioral + physiological check-in indices. Displayed converted to the PHQ-9 scale (0–27) so all severity numbers on this dashboard share one scale.
Regulatory status: FDA ISTAND — Letter of Intent stage only; not FDA-qualified.
Performance: ICC 0.82–0.92 (company-asserted; not independently verified; validation trials SEQUOIA-2/-3 ongoing).
Known limitations: accuracy degrades with <70% check-in completeness; not validated for acute crisis assessment.

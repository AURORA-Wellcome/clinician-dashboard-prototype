Design mockups

What would you want to know before each session?

AURORA turns brief voice check-ins and phone sensing into a regular estimate of depression severity. These mockups are our answer so far: who needs attention first, what has changed, and what’s worth talking about with the patient. Tell us what we’ve missed.

For review by the clinician working group. All patient data are fictional.

3

Latest version, October 24, 2026

Progress-focused

Built around one question: is each patient improving as expected?

  • Flags patients who aren’t improving, not only those getting worse
  • Puts the patient’s own PHQ-9 first, beside the AI estimate
  • Shows sleep, activity and contact on the same weeks as the trend
  • Opens any check-in: what came up, and what the protocol asks after a safety flag (draft)
Open v3

How to review

  1. Open v3The latest version starts on the caseload, the list of a clinician’s patients.
  2. Try the 5-second testLook at the caseload for five seconds. Who needs attention first?
  3. Switch optionsThe striped strip at the top changes pages and design options. It isn’t part of the design. Its “All versions” link brings you back here.
  4. Leave notesClick Notes at bottom right, here or in a mockup. Notes are shared only through the private review link from the study team. The link has an access code in it. On this public site, notes stay in your browser.

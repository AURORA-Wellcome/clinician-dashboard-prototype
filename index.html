AURORA Clinician dashboard GitHub

Design mockups

See how each patient is doing between visits.

AURORA turns brief voice check-ins and phone sensing into a regular estimate of depression severity. These mockups explore how that picture could reach clinicians: who needs attention first, what has changed, and what is worth talking about with the patient.

For review by the clinician working group · All patient data are fictional

How to review

  1. Open a versionEach one starts on the caseload, the list of a clinician’s patients.
  2. Try the 5-second testLook at the caseload for five seconds. Who needs attention first?
  3. Switch optionsThe striped strip at the top changes pages and design options. It isn’t part of the design.

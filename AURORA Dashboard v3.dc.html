Model Facts: AI-COA (Deliberate AI)

Intended use
Severity context to supplement the PHQ-9 and clinical judgment, never to replace either.
Output
A severity score on the PHQ-9 scale (0–27), with an uncertainty interval. It comes from check-in interviews and phone sensors.
Changes
A change is colored when it is 6 or more points from the patient’s own baseline (their typical level in their first weeks in AURORA).
Regulatory status
FDA ISTAND letter of intent only. Not FDA-qualified.
Performance
ICC 0.82–0.92, reported by the company. Not independently verified; validation is ongoing.
Known limits
Less accurate below 9 of 12 check-ins in 14 days. Not validated for crisis assessment.

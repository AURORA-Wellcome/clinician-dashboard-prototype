Model Facts: AI-COA (Deliberate AI)

Intended use
Supplementary severity context alongside the PHQ-9 and clinical judgment. Never a replacement for either.
Output
A severity score with an uncertainty interval, from check-in interviews and phone sensors, shown on the PHQ-9 scale (0–27).
Changes
A change is coloured when it is 6 or more points from the patient’s own baseline (their typical level in their first weeks in AURORA).
Regulatory status
FDA ISTAND letter of intent only. Not FDA-qualified.
Performance
ICC 0.82–0.92, reported by the company. Not independently verified; validation is ongoing.
Known limits
Less accurate below 9 of 12 check-ins in 14 days. Not validated for crisis assessment.
